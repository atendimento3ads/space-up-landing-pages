# Space Up Construction

> Space Up Construction is based in Natick, Massachusetts. These pages describe its flooring, residential construction, remodeling, siding, painting, and finishing services.

## Services

- [Epoxy flooring](https://services.spaceupconstruction.com/epoxy-flooring/): Garage and commercial concrete flooring, with flake, metallic, quartz, and solid color finishes. Preparation and installation timing depend on the existing concrete and selected flooring system.
- [Home construction and remodeling](https://services.spaceupconstruction.com/home-construction/): New residential construction and remodeling. The property, scope, and project requirements determine the next steps.
- [Siding, painting, and finishing](https://services.spaceupconstruction.com/siding-finishing/): Massachusetts residential siding installation and replacement; interior and exterior painting; drywall repair, plaster, and finish carpentry.
- [Services overview](https://services.spaceupconstruction.com/): Links to all service pages and business contact information.
